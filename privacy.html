Privacy Policy for Mitt Innbo

In short: we collect nothing. Everything you register stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Mitt Innbo has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no third-party services. The app sends no data over the internet.

Data on your phone

  • Rooms, items, prices, photos and receipts are stored only in the app's own encrypted storage on your phone.
  • Recognizing objects in photos and text on receipts happens on the phone. Photos are never uploaded.
  • Photos are saved without location (GPS) or other metadata.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph rooms, items and receipts. These photos are not added to your photo library.
  • Photos: the app uses the iOS photo picker and only sees the photos you choose.
  • Face ID (optional): to lock the app. Face ID is handled by Apple; the app never gets face data.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone (to iCloud or a computer), the app's data may be included. That is controlled by you and Apple; we have no access to it.

Sharing

Data only leaves your phone if you share the PDF report yourself, for example with your insurance company. The temporary report file is deleted when you close the report.

Apple

Downloads from the App Store are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. If you chose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete All Data" in the app's Settings, or delete the app. Everything is then removed from your phone.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email mittinnbo@gmail.com.

Last updated 7 November 2026. If we change this policy, the new version will be published here.
